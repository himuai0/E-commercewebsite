## Choose The Right Printing Method

- **DTF Printing:** good for colourful designs, names, numbers and flexible quantities.
- **Silkscreen:** good for larger bulk T-shirt orders with simple artwork and strong colours.
- **Embroidery:** good for premium logos on polo shirts, uniforms, caps and jackets.
- **Sublimation:** good for selected polyester sportswear, jerseys and full-colour designs.

## What To Know Before Ordering Custom T-Shirts In Malaysia

Before ordering custom T-shirts, decide how the shirts will be used. Event shirts usually need clear artwork, visible branding and a confirmed deadline. Company shirts may need a more professional material, consistent sizing and logo placement. School, club and team orders often need names, numbers, colour options and size planning.

For most buyers, the important details are quantity, material, printing position, artwork colour, delivery date and budget. TMaker can recommend a suitable T-shirt and printing method after reviewing those details.

### For Companies

Choose polo shirts, round neck T-shirts or uniforms depending on staff usage and brand image.

### For Events

Choose comfortable T-shirts with clear branding and plan production time before the event date.

### For Bulk Orders

Confirm quantity, size breakdown and artwork early so quotation and production can move faster.

### For Printing Method

Use DTF for colourful artwork, silkscreen for larger simple designs, and embroidery for premium logos.
