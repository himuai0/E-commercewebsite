## Bulk T-Shirt Printing Planning Guide

Bulk T-shirt orders need more planning than small orders because size breakdown, artwork approval, packing and delivery timing can affect the whole project. The best way to avoid delay is to confirm your quantity, colour, deadline and artwork before requesting final production.

For corporate events and campaigns, it is better to prepare one main contact person, one approved artwork file and one final quantity list. This helps TMaker quote faster and reduces changes after approval.

### Best For Bulk

Company events, campaign shirts, roadshows, school activities, clubs, teams and large giveaways.

### Best Method

Silkscreen is often suitable for larger simple artwork. DTF is useful for colourful and detailed designs.

### What To Prepare

Quantity, size list, artwork file, shirt colour, printing position, deadline and delivery location.

### How To Save Time

Send your design and quantity early so TMaker can recommend the right shirt and printing method.
